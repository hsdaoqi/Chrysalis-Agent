### 最有价值评论（作者：Gomotono）

> "I don't think this is true at all. It might feel like this because we are used to a very very fast release cycle but we are only in this topic for a few years. We have so many ways of optimizing:
> - We are getting better hardware that optimizes training and inference for these models
> - We are getting new architectures that require less tokens and computation
> - We are seeing agents and routing mechanisms that can use small and large models together
> - We are in the middle of the biggest Reinforcement loop with all the training data we give them day to day"
>
> ——Gomotono 认为 LLM 的发展远未到瓶颈，硬件、架构、agent 路由和日常强化学习都在推动进步。
